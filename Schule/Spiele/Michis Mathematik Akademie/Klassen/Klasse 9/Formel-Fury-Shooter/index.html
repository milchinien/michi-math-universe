HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Goal

Eliminate alien enemies by solving binomial formulas! Build high combos and collect points to unlock new formula types.

🎮 Controls

  • WASD - Move
  • Mouse - Aim at and click enemies
  • SPACE - Open/close formula HUD
  • ESC - Pause menu
  • Enter - Submit answer

🧮 Formula Types

  • 1st binomial: (a+b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
  • 2nd binomial: (a-b)² = a² - 2ab + b²
  • 3rd binomial: (a+b)(a-b) = a² - b²
  • Factoring: The binomial formulas in reverse

🔥 Combo-System

Correct answers in a row raise your combo! High combos give bonus points and unlock tougher enemies.

📈 Progression

As your score and combo level rise, you unlock new formula types and face stronger enemies. Stay alert!
